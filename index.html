Install Meds Tracker as an App

You're currently using Meds Tracker in Safari. For a better experience, install it as a standalone app on your home screen!

How to Install:

  1. Tap the Share button
    Look for the ⎋ icon at the bottom of Safari (iPhone) or top (iPad)
  2. Scroll and tap "Add to Home Screen"
    You may need to scroll down in the share menu to find this option
  3. Tap "Add"
    The app will appear on your home screen with its icon

Transfer Your Data:

If you already have medications and data in Safari:

  1. Before installing, go to Settings → Export Data
  2. Save the backup file to Files app or iCloud Drive
  3. After installing the app, open it and go to Settings → Import Data
  4. Select your backup file to restore everything

Benefits:

  • Faster access from your home screen
  • Full-screen experience without browser bars
  • Works like a native app
  • Separate from Safari browsing
